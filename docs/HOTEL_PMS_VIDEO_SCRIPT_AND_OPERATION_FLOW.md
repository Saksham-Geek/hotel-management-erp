### Video Scene 8: Statutory Aadhaar Masking & Data Privacy
* **Visual on Screen:** Receptionist clicks **"🛡️ Mask / Redact ID"**. The Aadhaar card appears on an interactive canvas. Receptionist clicks **"⚡ Mask Aadhaar (8 Digits & QR)"**. Instant solid pitch-black boxes burn over the first 8 digits and the QR barcode, leaving only the last 4 digits visible.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "Under Indian law, storing unmasked Aadhaar numbers is illegal. Our built-in ID Redaction canvas automatically detects and blacks out the first eight digits and QR code on the browser canvas before saving — keeping your hotel 100% compliant with UIDAI regulations with zero manual photo editing."
* **Step-by-Step Instructions:**
  1. Click **"Mask / Redact ID"** on any uploaded identity card.
  2. Click **"⚡ Mask Aadhaar (8 Digits & QR)"** (or drag custom blackout boxes over sensitive details).
  3. Click **"✓ Burn Redactions & Save"**. The compliant file is permanently stored as a masked document.

* **Visual on Screen:** Guest is checking out. The receptionist opens `/billing`. The folio shows room charges (₹8,000) and restaurant dining (₹1,450) with SAC codes and 12% / 5% GST calculated automatically. Receptionist selects **"Dynamic UPI QR"** and clicks **"🖥️ Fullscreen Counter QR"**. A high-contrast payment screen displays on the guest-facing counter screen. Guest scans with PhonePe. A green checkmark pulses with a chime: *Payment Received · UTR #32891024*. Receptionist clicks **"Send WhatsApp"** — guest's phone buzzes with the official PDF invoice.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "Settling a bill is seamless. The cashiering module combines room tariffs, dining, and minibar orders into a consolidated folio with dual-slab Indian GST. Click 'Fullscreen Counter QR' to display a dynamic payment QR code directly on your counter monitor. The moment the guest pays on GPay or PhonePe, the system verifies the bank UTR, marks the folio settled, and dispatches the tax invoice straight to the guest's WhatsApp."
* **Step-by-Step Instructions:**
  1. Open `/billing` and select the guest's room folio.
  2. Choose payment method:
     - **UPI Dynamic QR**: Click *Fullscreen Counter Display* for the guest to scan.
     - **EDC Card Terminal**: Choose Pine Labs, Paytm, or Innoviti to push the charge to your physical card swiper.
     - **Cash**: Enter amount received (system alerts if cash exceeds ₹2 Lakh under Section 269ST).
  3. Once settled, click **"💬 WhatsApp Tax Invoice"** to send the GST bill directly to the guest's WhatsApp.
  4. Click **"Print 80mm Thermal Slip"** for a paper receipt.
